Ochre began in a borrowed print shop with one belief: design is a public art. Each June an old warehouse on the river becomes a lecture hall, a studio and a street gallery — open to anyone who makes things look right.

  • 4days of talks, workshops and night screenings
  • 60speakers and studios from twenty-one countries
  • 9venues along the river, a short walk apart

Six ways to spend four days

Mix them as you like. Every pass opens the talks and the print fair; workshops and reviews are booked ahead.

  • Talks Forty-minute stories from the studios that shaped the year: how the work was made, what went wrong, what they would do again. Daily · Main hall
  • Workshops Twelve people, one long table. Type design, risograph, identity sprints and photo editing with the people who teach it best. Thu–Sat · Book ahead
  • Open studios Twenty local studios open their doors. Walk in, see the pin-up walls, ask the questions you would never ask on a call. Fri–Sat · Two routes
  • Print fair Posters, zines and books from eighty independent makers. Bring a tube, some cash and an empty shelf at home. Thu–Sun · Hall B
  • Night screenings Films about designers and films designers love, projected on the warehouse wall after dark. Thu & Sat · 21:30
  • Portfolio reviews Twenty minutes with an art director who has hired before. Bring your work, leave with a plan. Fri–Sun · By booking

A warehouse on the river

Armazém Nove stored cork for a hundred years. Now its brick, steel and river light hold every talk, workshop and print table — and the terrace stays open until midnight.

Before you ask

Can I get a refund?

Passes are refundable until the first of May. After that you can hand yours to a friend — write to us with their name and we will change the badge.

Is the venue accessible?

Yes. The warehouse is on one level with step-free entrances, accessible toilets on both sides and quiet rooms upstairs, reached by a lift.

Do I need to book workshops?

Yes, each one takes twelve people. Festival and patron passes include two bookings; anyone else can add one for €25.

Where should I stay?

We keep a short list of guesthouses within a walk of the river, with a festival rate until the end of April. Ask and we will send it.

Can I volunteer?

We need forty volunteers every year. Four shifts earn you a festival pass, lunch every day and a very good T-shirt.

Are children welcome?

Under-twelves come free with an adult, and Saturday morning has a poster workshop just for them.
